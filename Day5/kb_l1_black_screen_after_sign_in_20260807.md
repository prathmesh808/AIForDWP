# Black Screen After You Sign In

Version: 1.0  
Date: 07/08/2026  
Status: Draft

If your screen stays black after you sign in, your files and data are safe. This problem may clear after a short time, and a few simple steps may help you start work again.

## What You Can Do

1. Wait for 30 seconds after you sign in.
Sometimes the screen appears after a short delay.

2. If the screen is still black, sign out.
If you can see a sign-out option, use it. If not, close the window and sign in again.

3. Sign in again once.
Do not keep trying many times in a row.

4. If the problem happens again, restart your own laptop or PC.
After restart, sign in again once.

5. If you can get to your normal work screen after trying again, save your work and keep watching for the same problem.
If the black screen comes back, contact the Service Desk.

## Before You Contact The Service Desk

Please note these details so the team can help faster:

- Your name and work email address.
- The time the problem happened.
- Whether the screen stayed black or appeared after a wait.
- Whether you were signed out automatically.
- Whether this happened once or every time you signed in.
- Whether restarting your laptop or PC helped.
- Your location, if you are working from home or office.
- A screenshot or photo, if you can take one safely.

## Important

- Your files, email, and saved work are safe.
- Do not change settings on your computer.
- Do not keep trying to sign in many times.

Contact the Service Desk if the screen stays black after one more sign-in attempt and one restart, if you are signed out again, or if you still cannot work.
