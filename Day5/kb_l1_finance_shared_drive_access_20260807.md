# Finance Shared Folder Access Help (L1)
# Version: v 1.0
# Date: 07/08/2026
# Status: Draft

If you cannot open the Finance shared folder (S: drive), do not worry. Your files are safe and are not deleted. This issue is usually temporary and can often be fixed with a fresh sign-in.

## What You Can Do Now

1. Save any open work on your computer.
Expected result: You are ready to safely sign out.

2. Sign out of Windows (not restart yet).
Expected result: You return to the sign-in screen.

3. Wait 60 seconds before signing back in.
Expected result: Your computer has time to refresh connections.

4. Sign back in with your normal work account.
Expected result: Your desktop opens normally.

5. Open File Explorer and select `This PC`.
Expected result: You can see your drives.

6. Open the `S:` drive.
Expected result: The Finance shared folder opens.

7. If `S:` is missing, wait 2 minutes and check again.
Expected result: The drive appears after background refresh.

8. If still not working, connect to your usual work network (office network or company VPN), then check `S:` again.
Expected result: The shared folder opens once network access is available.

## Important Reassurance

- Your data is safe.
- This issue does not delete your Finance files.
- The folder remains on the company server even if it is not visible for a short time.

## Contact the Service Desk if...

Contact the Service Desk if the `S:` drive is still missing or cannot be opened after one sign-out/sign-in cycle and a 2-minute wait.

## Details to Include in Your Ticket

- Your full name and work email
- Your device name (if known)
- The exact time the issue started
- What you see: `S:` missing or error message text
- Whether you are on office network or VPN
- Whether sign out/sign in was already tried
- Whether other shared folders open normally
