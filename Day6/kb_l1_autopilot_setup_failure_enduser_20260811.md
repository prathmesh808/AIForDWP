# Help Guide: My computer stopped setting itself up — what should I do?

---

## Your data is safe

If your computer stopped during first-time setup, your files, emails, and account have not been lost or changed. This is a setup issue only — nothing has been deleted and no one has accessed your account.

---

## What is happening?

When you receive a new work computer, or one that has been given to you again, it runs an automatic process that gets the computer ready for you the first time you switch it on. Occasionally this process stops before it finishes and shows a problem screen. This is usually caused by an old record still being linked to your computer from a previous setup, which prevents the new setup from completing.

This is a known issue and the IT team can fix it quickly.

---

## What you can try yourself

**Step 1 — Do not switch the computer off at the wall or hold the power button.**
If the setup screen has frozen or shows a problem, leave the computer switched on and on that screen. Switching it off this way can make things harder to fix.

**Step 2 — Wait 5 minutes.**
Sometimes the setup screen takes a little longer to move on. If it has not changed after 5 minutes, move to Step 3.

**Step 3 — Contact the IT Service Desk** (see the section below).
Do not try to restart the computer, click through any problem messages, or type any details on the setup screen. Leave it exactly as it is and call the IT Service Desk or send a request to the IT team.

---

## Contact the IT Service Desk if...

- The setup screen shows a problem and has not moved for more than 5 minutes
- The computer keeps returning to the same screen asking for your name or password after you try to continue
- You are unsure whether the setup finished successfully

---

## What to tell the Service Desk

Having the following details ready will help the team fix this faster:

- [ ] The name printed on the computer — usually on a sticker on the lid or base (e.g. DESKTOP-FB099)
- [ ] A unique number printed on a sticker on the base of the laptop or back of the desktop — this is sometimes labelled "S/N" or "Serial"
- [ ] Your full work email address (e.g. yourname@finbridge.com)
- [ ] A description of what the screen shows — if there is a number on the problem screen, read it out exactly as shown
- [ ] Whether the computer is plugged in to power and connected to the internet (Wi-Fi or cable)

The Service Desk will take it from there. You do not need to do anything else.
