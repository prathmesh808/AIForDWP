Subject: Known issue: Credentials Vault Access

Team,

We are aware of an issue where some users cannot access the shared credentials vault. This is a known incident and our support teams are actively working on it now.

As an interim step, please try:
1. Sign out of the vault page completely, close your browser, then open it again and sign in fresh.
2. If it still fails, try once in a private/incognito window to rule out a saved browser issue.

If access is still not working after these steps, please contact the Service Desk and share your name, team, the vault link used, the exact time you tried, any error message, and a screenshot.

Thank you for your patience. We know this is disruptive, and we are treating it as a priority. We will post the next update by 4:00 PM today, even if there is no final fix yet.
