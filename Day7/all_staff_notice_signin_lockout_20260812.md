# All-Staff Notice — Sign-In & Account Access
**Date:** 2026-08-12
**From:** IT Service Desk
**To:** All FinBridge Staff

---

We are aware that a number of colleagues are experiencing difficulty signing in or finding their account locked this morning. We want to reassure you that this is a known issue being actively investigated by the IT team right now.

**While we work on a fix, please try the following:**

- Wait 15 minutes and attempt to sign in again — accounts unlock automatically after a short time.
- If you are asked for a verification code, check your mobile phone or backup email and enter it when prompted.

If you are still unable to sign in after trying the above, please contact the Service Desk and have the following ready: your name, your username, the time it happened, and any error message you saw on screen.

We will send an update to all staff by **14:00 today**. We appreciate your patience — the team is working to resolve this as quickly as possible.
