Credentials Vault Access
Try these steps:
1. Sign out of the vault page completely, close your browser, then open it again and sign in fresh.
2. If it still fails, try once in a private/incognito window to rule out a saved browser issue.

If you still cannot open the vault after these two steps, contact the Service Desk and share: your name, team, the exact time you tried, the vault link you used, any error message shown, and a screenshot.

Admin Console Lockout
Try these steps:
1. Sign out of all company apps, then sign in again and retry the admin console from your normal company portal link.
2. If locked out again, restart your device once and try one more sign-in.

If you are still locked out after these steps, contact the Service Desk and share: your name, team, the console link used, the lockout message text, the time it happened, and a screenshot for faster resolution.
