# FinBridge Win 11 Migration — Post-Migration Feedback Theme Analysis
**Date:** 2026-08-12
**Analyst:** DWP Migration Review
**Source:** 50 post-migration end-user comments, FinBridge staff

---

## Top 3 Priority Actions — Ranked by Severity + Volume

### Rank 1 — Account Lockout / Sign-in Failure
**Count:** 7 comments (IDs: 1, 11, 16, 21, 29, 37, 45) | **Severity: Blocker**

**Why it ranks here:** Highest volume of Blocker-severity comments in the dataset. Users are fully locked out — no workaround exists. Repeat lockouts (up to three times in one week for the same individual) indicate a systemic issue, not a one-off. Occurring before important client calls compounds the business impact.

**Manager summary:** Seven FinBridge staff have been fully locked out of their accounts since migration, with repeat occurrences suggesting an underlying policy or MFA configuration issue that needs same-day investigation.

---

### Rank 2 — OneDrive Files Missing / Sync Errors
**Count:** 4 comments (IDs: 14, 23, 34, 42) | **Severity: Blocker**

**Why it ranks here:** Although lower volume than Rank 1, perceived data loss is among the highest-risk outcomes of any migration. Multiple users report files not present after checking multiple times, with named deadlines (Q1 report, end-of-day meeting). Data integrity risk and user anxiety make this urgent regardless of whether files are truly lost or simply not yet synced.

**Manager summary:** Four users believe their OneDrive files are missing post-migration, two with hard deadlines today — this must be validated immediately to rule out data loss before it escalates to a formal complaint.

---

### Rank 3 — VPN Instability During Calls
**Count:** 4 comments (IDs: 5, 24, 39, 47) | **Severity: Blocker**

**Why it ranks here:** VPN drops during live client calls are externally visible failures — they damage FinBridge's professional reputation, not just internal productivity. One user reports four drops in a single hour. This outranks the Finance Shared Drive theme (also Blocker, 3 comments) due to equal volume with higher reputational stakes.

**Manager summary:** VPN is dropping repeatedly during client calls — up to four times in one hour — which is creating a visible professionalism issue that needs network/infrastructure investigation today.
