# FinBridge Win 11 Migration — Self-Service Guidance (Top 3 Issues)
**Date:** 2026-08-12

---

## 1. Cannot Log In / Account Locked

Wait 15 minutes and try signing in again — accounts unlock automatically after a short time.
If you are prompted for a verification code, check your mobile phone or backup email and enter it when asked.

**Still locked out? Contact the Service Desk.**
Tell them: your full name, your username, the time it happened, and whether you saw any error message on screen.

---

## 2. OneDrive Files Appear Missing

Click the OneDrive cloud icon in the bottom-right corner of your screen. If it shows a spinning circle or a red cross, it is still syncing — wait 5 minutes and check again.
If the icon is not there, open the Start menu, search for **OneDrive**, and open it to restart the sync.

**Files still missing after 10 minutes? Contact the Service Desk.**
Tell them: your name, which folder or file is missing, and the last time you could see it.

---

## 3. VPN Keeps Dropping

Disconnect from the VPN completely, wait 30 seconds, then reconnect. This clears most temporary connection faults.
If you are on Wi-Fi, try moving closer to your router or switching to a wired connection if one is available.

**Still dropping after reconnecting? Contact the Service Desk.**
Tell them: your name, how many times it has dropped today, what you were doing when it dropped (e.g. on a call), and whether you are on Wi-Fi or wired.
