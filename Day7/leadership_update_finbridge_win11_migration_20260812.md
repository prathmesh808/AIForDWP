# Leadership Update — FinBridge Win 11 Migration
**Date:** 2026-08-12
**For:** Senior Management
**From:** IT Service Desk / DWP Migration Team

---

The Windows 11 migration for FinBridge staff has gone well overall. The majority of colleagues are now on the new platform with no issues, and positive feedback has been consistent across both weeks — staff have noted faster machines, better battery life, and a smooth experience. The problems that did emerge in the first week — including colleagues being locked out of their accounts, missing files in OneDrive, VPN instability during client calls, and access issues with the finance shared drive — were all resolved within the week. Login speeds, which had slowed noticeably for some users after the migration, have also returned to normal. The IT team moved quickly on these issues and the improvement between week one and week two is clear.

Two issues remain open and are being actively managed. The floor 3 printer has not mapped correctly since the migration began and, despite ongoing effort, is still unresolved going into week three — staff on that floor are currently using the floor 2 printer as a workaround, but frustration is growing and this is being escalated. Separately, a new issue has emerged this week where colleagues in the finance team are experiencing their computers becoming noticeably slower to be ready for use in the mornings. This has been traced to a security update that was applied to finance devices on 4 August, and the team is now investigating which part of that update is responsible so it can be adjusted without removing the security protections it provides. Both issues have owners and are being prioritised.
