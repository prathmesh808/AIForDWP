The migration has broadly delivered the expected user experience, but the first business-day wave on Floor 6 exposed a few high-impact pain points that affected confidence. Most users are now able to work normally after targeted fixes, and there is no indication of data loss from the desktop or sign-in disruptions. Where users experienced slow access, missing desktop icons, or confusion around what Copilot surfaced, the issues were linked to how access settings and device state were applied in practice rather than a broad platform outage. In plain terms: the migration foundation is sound, but early-day operational friction was real and visible to end users.

What remains open is the access-boundary review for the Copilot "unexpected client matter" case and final confirmation that access lines match legal expectations in every scenario. That work is in progress with legal and security oversight, evidence capture, and controlled retesting before final closure. In parallel, we have put stronger guardrails in place: earlier checks before rollout, live monitoring during rollout, faster rollback triggers, and clearer service-desk playbooks for frontline support. The focus now is to reduce repeat disruption, speed up response when issues appear, and complete formal sign-off on the remaining confidentiality-sensitive item without compromising business continuity.
