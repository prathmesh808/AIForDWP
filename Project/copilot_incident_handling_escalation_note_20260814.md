# The Copilot Incident — Handled Correctly

## What It Actually Is
This is a potential confidentiality and access-boundary incident, not a normal product "odd behavior" ticket. It must be handled as a security-governed access investigation: preserve prompt/response/citation evidence, contain further exposure for the reporting user while maintaining business continuity, and validate effective access and inheritance paths with Legal and Security oversight before any closure decision.

## What We Would NOT Do
1. Do not close it as "AI weirdness" or user misunderstanding without evidence.
2. Do not rely on memory or screenshots alone and skip source/citation validation.
3. Do not make broad permission changes first and investigate later.
4. Do not communicate "resolved" before access-path verification and controlled retest.

## Two-Sentence Escalation Draft
"A potential confidentiality-boundary incident has been identified: Copilot returned a client matter that the reporting user states they should not be able to access. We have initiated containment and evidence capture, and request immediate Legal, Security, and platform-owner escalation to validate effective access paths and confirm whether this is a permission boundary issue or a product enforcement defect."
