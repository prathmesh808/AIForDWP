If Copilot shows client content you did not expect to see, stay calm. The IT help team is actively checking it.

Your files and data are safe.

## What you can safely do now

1. Stop using that Copilot conversation for now.
2. Do not share or forward the response to others.
3. Take a photo of your screen showing the answer (if allowed by your team policy).
4. Copy the exact question you typed.
5. Copy any links or reference names shown with the answer.
6. Note the exact time this happened.
7. Contact the IT help team immediately and say this is a potential confidential-content access issue.
8. Do not retry the same Copilot prompt until the IT help team confirms it is safe.

## What to include in your help request

Please share these details when you contact the IT help team:

- Your full name
- Your work email
- Your floor/location (Floor 6)
- The exact time you saw the issue
- The exact question you entered
- The response text you received
- Any links or reference names shown
- A photo of your screen (if allowed)
- Best phone number to reach you

## Contact the IT help team if...

Contact the IT help team if Copilot shows unexpected client content again, or if you are unsure whether the result should be visible to you.
