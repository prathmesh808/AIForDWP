If login is failing or taking a long time this morning, you are not alone. We know this is affecting multiple people, and the help desk team is actively working on it.

Your files and work data are safe.

## What you can do right now

1. Stay calm and wait 2 minutes on the login screen.
2. If nothing changes, choose Restart on your computer.
3. After restart, try logging in once using your work email and password.
4. If login still fails, wait 5 minutes and try one more time.
5. If you can log in but everything is very slow, save your work, log out once, and then log in again.
6. If you are using office Wi-Fi, stay connected and do not switch Wi-Fi while trying again.
7. Do not keep trying many times in a row. Trying too many times quickly can make it take longer to fix.

## While you wait

- Keep this help page open.
- Be ready to share simple details with the help desk.
- Avoid changing settings you are not familiar with.

## Details to include in your help request

Please copy and share these details when you contact the help desk:

- Your full name
- Your work email
- Your floor/location (Floor 6)
- Computer name (if visible on a sticker or login screen)
- Exact time the issue happened
- What you saw: "could not log in" or "login very slow"
- Any message shown on screen (exact words or photo)
- Whether a restart was tried
- Whether the second login try worked
- Best contact number

## Contact the service desk if...

Contact the service desk if you still cannot log in after one restart and two tries, or if login remains very slow for more than 10 minutes.
