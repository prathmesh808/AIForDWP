If your desktop icons are missing this morning, you are not alone. We know this is affecting some people on Floor 6, and the IT help team is working on it.

Your files and data are safe.

## What you can safely do now

1. Wait 2 minutes on your desktop screen.
2. Click the right mouse button on an empty area of your desktop and select Refresh.
3. If icons are still missing, save any open work.
4. Log out once, then log back in.
5. Wait another 2 minutes after signing in.
6. If icons are still missing, restart your computer once.
7. After restart, sign in again and check your desktop.

Please do not change settings you are not familiar with.

## What to include in your help request

When you contact the IT help team, share these details:

- Your full name
- Your work email
- Your floor/location (Floor 6)
- Your computer name (if visible)
- The time you first noticed missing icons
- Whether all icons are missing or only some
- The name of one missing icon you use most
- Whether you already tried log out/log in
- Whether you already tried restart
- A photo of your screen (if possible)
- Best phone number to reach you

## Contact the IT help team if...

Contact the IT help team if icons are still missing after one log out/log in and one restart, or if you cannot open your usual work programs from desktop icons.
