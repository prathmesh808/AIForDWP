# Floor 6 Update for Partners (By Lunch)

## What Happened
This morning, Floor 6 had three separate issues at the same time. Some people had trouble signing in or had very slow access. Some people found that desktop icons they use for daily work were missing. One person also reported that Copilot showed client content they did not expect to see. This created understandable concern and disrupted work at the start of the day.

There is no evidence of data loss from the sign-in or desktop icon issues.

## What Is Being Done Right Now
We treated this as a priority business-impact event and worked issue by issue.

For sign-in delays and failures, we stabilized access and applied targeted fixes so people could get back to work.

For missing desktop icons, we restored normal desktop behavior and put back required shortcuts where needed.

For the Copilot report, we immediately treated it as sensitive. We contained risk, captured evidence, and started a full access review with legal and security oversight.

At the same time, we strengthened our response process so front-line teams can act faster if this pattern appears again.

## What Is Still Open
Two of the three issues are stabilized and operating normally for affected users.

One item remains open: the Copilot access-boundary review. We are confirming that access lines match legal expectations in all cases before final closure.

## What Happens Next
We will complete the remaining review, share final findings, and confirm closure criteria with Legal and Security.

We are also putting a stricter early-morning readiness checkpoint in place before future business-day starts, so issues of this type are caught before users are impacted.

## Bottom Line
Business service has been stabilized, risk has been contained, and the remaining sensitive item is under active controlled review.
